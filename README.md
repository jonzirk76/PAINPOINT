Controls (Controller):

Left stick to move

Right stick to shoot, there isn't autoshooting, the player shoots whenever the stick state changes

Hold left trigger to use overdrive ammo (ammo upgrades are received after challenge rooms and bosses)

Hold right trigger when super bar is flashing to charge a charge shot.

Press z to parry, parries close to the character refresh the parry cooldown.

Stand next to bullets to reduce parry cooldown.
